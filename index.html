How scoring works

Two separate numbers, because a story can be slanted without being spun, and spun without being partisan.

Leaning −100 → +100Where the framing, sourcing and word choice sit on the left/right spectrum for the topic at hand. Zero is neutral reporting, not a mathematical average of opinions.
Spin 0 → 100How far the story pushes the reader toward a conclusion beyond what its facts support: loaded words, one-sided sourcing, missing context, headline-body mismatch.
Evidence requiredEvery score cites quoted passages and says why each one counts. If the model can't point to text, it can't lower or raise a score.

Analyze it yourself

Paste a headline and a few paragraphs of any political story. Claude returns a leaning, a spin score and the evidence.

Automated scoring is a judgment call and can be wrong. Treat it as a prompt to read critically, not a verdict.
